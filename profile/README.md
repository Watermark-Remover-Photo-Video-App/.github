## 📖 Introduction
Watermark Remover for Windows is an AI watermark remover for anyone who wants to clean their own images and clips. Whether a stray timestamp is sitting in the corner of a holiday photo or a logo overlaps a video you recorded, its AI inpainting engine studies the surrounding pixels and rebuilds the covered area convincingly. Beyond removal, it can also apply and manage your own watermarks, giving you one editor for both cleaning up and branding the media you own.

## 🔆 Highlights

### Cleanup and restoration
- **AI inpainting** — Removes watermarks, logos, and timestamps from your own photos and reconstructs the background so the fix looks seamless.
- **Object removal** — Clear unwanted objects from your own images; this AI watermark remover fills the gap based on the surrounding scene.
- **Single-image cleanup** — Erase a watermark from your own image in a couple of steps, a simple option when you need a quick fix.

### Video and branding
- **Video watermarks** — Acts as a video watermark remover for clips you own, following the mark across frames for a clean result.
- **Your own watermarks** — Add, resize, and reposition your personal or brand watermark, then clean it up again whenever you choose.
